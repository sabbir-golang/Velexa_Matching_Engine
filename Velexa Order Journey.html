    The three roads

    Orders go out

    Trader screenWebSocketOMS APIKafkaFIX InitiatorFIX 35=DMatching engine

    The OMS checks the broker's risk limits and the client's money first. Only orders that pass leave the broker.

    Reports come back

    Matching engineFIX 35=8FIX InitiatorWebSocketOMS APIWebSocketTrader screen

    One report for every change: accepted, each trade, cancelled, rejected, expired. The OMS updates the order, cash and portfolio.

    Prices go to everyone

    Matching engineITCHITCH EngineWebSocket :9001Every trader screen

    Every order added, traded or removed is published. The ITCH Engine stores prices in MongoDB and streams them straight to the screens.
